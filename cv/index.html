Professional Summary

Ph.D. candidate at Peking University (expected 2027) working at the intersection of fluid-structure simulation and embedded sensing, with 3+ years of prior industry R&D experience as a CFD/CAE engineer in aerospace, renewable energy, and fluid machinery. On the simulation side, I specialize in aerodynamic and hydrodynamic shape optimization, FSI and FEM analysis, and surrogate-based design optimization. On the sensing side, I develop embedded piezoelectric (PVDF) sensing for soft bodies in flow, from signal-conditioning hardware and synchronized data acquisition to machine-learning estimation of body shape, flow conditions, and position, deployed in real time in water-channel tests.
