**Goal.** Harvest energy from water flow with a flexible, bio-inspired hydrofoil that carries an embedded PVDF piezoelectric film, and find the foil geometry that maximizes the harvested power.

**Approach.**

- Built a parametric CFD framework with two-way fluid-structure coupling in COMSOL Multiphysics, linking the flow, the structural deformation, and the piezoelectric response.
- Trained surrogate models on the simulation results and used them to drive the geometry optimization.
- Fabricated the baseline and optimized foils with the PVDF film embedded inside, then tested both in a water channel and in a natural stream.

**Results.**

- The optimized geometry increased the predicted harvested power by up to 122% over the baseline foil.
- In the water channel, the fabricated optimized foil delivered up to 30% higher measured electrical output.
- The harvester was also deployed successfully in a natural stream.

**Publication.** M. Hamza et al., "A Geometric Optimization Framework for a Bio-inspired Flexible Hydrofoil Energy Harvester," _Energy_, 2026. [doi:10.1016/j.energy.2026.140825](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2026.140825)
